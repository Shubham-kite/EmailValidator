    What this check can tell you

    Syntax: checks the address structure and domain format.

    MX records: checks whether DNS lists mail servers for the domain.

    Risk flags: matches a small built-in list of disposable providers and role names.

    Not checked: mailbox existence, SMTP delivery, and BriteVerify status require a trusted server-side provider.
